A place to begin

Good care begins with a conversation.

At Shadab Dental Care, your comfort, questions and goals shape every visit. Here’s who we are, what to expect, and answers to the questions patients ask most.

Meet your dentist

The team at Shadab Dental Care

We’re a patient-first clinic that takes the time to listen, explain and plan your care together with you. A full introduction to our dentist and team is coming soon.

01 / Before you visit

What should I bring?

Recent dental records or X-rays if you have them, a list of medicines you take, and any questions you’d like to ask. Arriving 10 minutes early gives you time to settle in.

02 / Your visit

What happens there?

We’ll talk through your concern, take a careful look, and explain what we find. Any treatment options are discussed with you before anything is done.

03 / After booking

Can I change my time?

Yes — cancel online from “My appointment” using your reference and phone number, then simply book a new time that suits you better.Yes — message the clinic on WhatsApp or give us a call, and we’ll move your visit to a time that suits you better.

Patient questions

Before you book.

Does an online booking confirm my appointment?

Yes. When you book an open time, it’s reserved for you immediately and you’ll see a confirmation with a unique booking reference. Keep it together with the phone number you entered.

Why do I need my reference and phone number?

Both are required so nobody can look up or cancel your appointment with the reference alone. Keep your reference private.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Open “My appointment”, enter your reference and phone number, and choose “Cancel appointment”. The time is released straight away, and you can book a new one.

How does booking work?

Choose the day and time you prefer, add a few details, and press “Send request on WhatsApp”. Your message opens in WhatsApp, ready to send. The clinic then replies to confirm your time or suggest another one.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Message the clinic on WhatsApp or give us a call, and we’ll move or cancel your visit.

What should I write in the description?

A short line is enough, like “check-up” or “pain in a back tooth when chewing”. Please don’t share highly sensitive medical details through the form — we’ll go through your history in person.

What if there are no times that suit me?

Try another day, or contact the clinic directly and we’ll find a time that works.

Is the online form for emergencies?

No. It isn’t monitored for urgent problems. For severe pain, swelling, bleeding that won’t stop, or a dental injury, phone the clinic or your local emergency services.

Can I book for my child?

Yes. Enter your own name and phone number, choose “Child’s visit” and mention your child’s name and age in the description.

Your information

Privacy, in plain language.

What we collect. Your name, phone number, optional email, the date and time you choose, the reason for your visit and the short description you write.

Why. Only to arrange, identify and prepare for your appointment, and to contact you about it if needed.

Where it’s kept. In the clinic’s own appointment database.This website stores nothing. Your details go only into the WhatsApp message you choose to send to the clinic. It isn’t sold or shared with advertisers, and this site uses no tracking cookies.

Your choices. You can cancel your booking online anytime. To have your details corrected or removed, contact the clinic.

Have a question we didn’t answer? Book a visit and ask us in person.
