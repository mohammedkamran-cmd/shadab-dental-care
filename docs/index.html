Good to know

A few things you might wonder.

All questions ↗

How do I book?

Choose a day and a time, add your name and phone number, and write a line about what you need. Your confirmation appears immediately.Your request then opens in WhatsApp, ready to send, and the clinic confirms your time there.

How do I change or cancel?

Just message the clinic on WhatsApp or give us a call, and we’ll move or cancel your visit.

Can I check or cancel my appointment later?

Yes. Go to “My appointment” and enter your booking reference with the phone number you booked with. You can see the details or cancel from there.

Is this form for emergencies?

No. The online form isn’t monitored for urgent problems. If you’re in severe pain, have swelling, or have had an injury, contact the clinic by phone or your local emergency services.

What should I bring?

Any recent dental records or X-rays if you have them, and a list of medicines you take.
